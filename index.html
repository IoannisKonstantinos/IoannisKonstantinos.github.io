Intrinsic Metric Time Dilation in Early and Late Cosmology

A Geometrodynamic Solution to the Horizon, Flatness, and Dark Energy Problems
Abstract

This work postulates a fundamental modification of the standard model of cosmology ($ \Lambda $CDM). By analytical coupling between local energy density $ \rho $ and the time metric $ g_{00} $, we eliminate the necessity for an Inflaton field.
